App name
Vision Simulator

Short description
Explore how myopia and astigmatism affect your view through a live camera.

Full description
What does the world look like with −6 diopters of myopia? Point your phone at a familiar scene and compare how it looks with and without simulated vision effects.

• Adjust myopia from −1 to −12 diopters.
• Add astigmatism and tune it independently, or try a hazy view.
• Tap 100% for the ordinary camera view, or 120% for a simulated boost in sharpness.
• On compatible devices, automatic depth uses Google Play Services for AR to vary the myopia effect between the foreground and background. Manual distance presets are also available.
• The interface is available in 20 languages.

Vision Simulator is an educational demonstration. It does not measure eyesight, diagnose eye conditions, or reproduce everyone’s vision exactly.

Release notes
