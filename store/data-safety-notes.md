# Play Console data safety notes

- Required app permission is Camera. There is no Internet, microphone, or location permission in the app manifest.
- The simulator uses the live image and depth data on the device. It has no photo/video export, user account, ads, or developer-operated analytics.
- Camera and manual filters remain available on devices without ARCore. On compatible devices with ARCore installed, the app starts an AR session for its viewfinder, including manual distance and ordinary-camera modes. ARCore requires collection by Google of account identifiers (or device identifiers when not signed in), performance and other diagnostics, API usage and app activity. Google stores this data for analytics and operating/improving AR services. Choosing a manual distance does not disable collection. The app uses neither Cloud Anchors nor Geospatial APIs, so their additional location/video collection does not apply.
- Disclose the applicable User IDs, Device or other IDs, App activity and App info/performance data types against the actual Play Console form and [the ARCore disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/play-safety-label). This is required collection during an AR session, not a user-controlled opt-in to depth. Google states this data is not shared with third parties and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS on devices originally shipped with Google Play Store. Check the form’s definitions and each purpose rather than assuming collection and sharing are interchangeable. Do not submit a blanket “No data collected” declaration.
- Publish `privacy-policy.html` at a public HTTPS URL. Developer: Georgiy (georrge1994). Privacy contact: Zhore-Chebotarev@mail.ru or https://github.com/georrge1994/vision-simulator/issues (issues are public; use email for private correspondence). Confirm the target audience, content questionnaire, countries, and production access in the intended account.
- The app is free of logins, ads, payments and access gates. It needs a camera permission grant to present the live simulation.
